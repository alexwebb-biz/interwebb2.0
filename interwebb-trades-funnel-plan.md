# Interwebb Trades Funnel Plan

## Objective

Position Interwebb to win more web design and development clients from trades businesses by adding a dedicated, conversion-focused landing page and using it as the core of a simple outbound funnel.

Your current site already establishes Interwebb as a modern studio for websites, web apps, design, development, and SEO. The main sales issue is not credibility. It is that the messaging is still broad. A trades business should be able to land on the site and immediately think: this is for me.

This plan narrows the message, improves the sales path, and gives you a brief you can hand directly to Codex.

---

## Why Trades Is a Good Niche

Trades is a good starting niche because:

- the value of a better website is easy to explain
- local trades businesses often have outdated or weak websites
- they care about calls, quote requests, and trust, not technical jargon
- you can make a simple, outcome-led offer
- it matches your existing strength in web development

Rather than trying to sell “web development for everyone”, the better offer is:

**Websites for trades businesses that want more calls, quote requests, and booked jobs.**

---

## Main Sales Improvements for Interwebb

The biggest improvements are not mainly visual. They are sales and positioning changes.

### 1. Add a niche page for trades

Create a dedicated page such as:

- `/web-design-for-trades`
- or `/trades-websites`

This page should clearly target plumbers, electricians, builders, roofers, landscapers, decorators, heating engineers, and similar businesses.

### 2. Lead with outcomes

Trades businesses are not buying “strategy, design, development, and SEO”.
They are buying:

- more quote requests
- more phone calls
- better trust
- a more professional first impression
- an easier way for customers to contact them from mobile

So the page should be outcome-led, not agency-led.

### 3. Offer a simple package

Give visitors a clear starting point such as:

**Trades Starter Website**
- up to 5 pages
- mobile-friendly
- contact / quote form
- click-to-call
- WhatsApp integration
- basic local SEO setup
- fast turnaround
- fixed-price option

This makes the service feel easier to buy.

### 4. Use one strong CTA

Use one consistent CTA across the page:

**Get a Free Website Audit**

That is stronger than a generic “Contact Us”.

### 5. Use a lead magnet

The best lead magnet here is:

**Free 5-minute website audit for trades businesses**

You can deliver this as:
- a short Loom video
- a written breakdown
- a quick scorecard

This gives you a good reason to do outbound and gives them a reason to enquire.

---

## Recommended Funnel

### Traffic sources

Use this page as the destination for:
- cold outreach to local trades businesses
- short Loom audits
- local networking outreach
- social content aimed at trades businesses
- future local SEO efforts

### Funnel structure

1. Prospect sees outreach or finds page
2. Visits trades landing page
3. Submits free audit form
4. Reaches thank-you page or success state
5. Receives audit
6. Books short call
7. Moves to fixed-price website offer
8. Optional upsell into support / SEO / maintenance

Keep it simple.

## 14-Day Action Plan

### Days 1–2
- add the trades landing page
- add the free audit form
- add thank-you state or thank-you page

### Days 3–4
- build a list of 50 local trades businesses with weak websites
- focus on businesses with outdated design, poor mobile experience, weak trust signals, or weak calls to action

### Days 5–14
Every day:
- contact 10 businesses
- offer the free website audit
- record 2–3 mini audits
- follow up after 2 days
- aim to book short calls

The first goal is not traffic. It is conversations.

## Main Principle

Do not overcomplicate this.

You do not need a huge funnel or multiple offers.

You need:
- one niche page
- one clear offer
- one clear CTA
- one simple form
- one outreach message
- consistent daily outreach

That is enough to start getting real conversations and turn Interwebb into a focused sales funnel for trades businesses.
